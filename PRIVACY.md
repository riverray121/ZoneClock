# ZoneClock Privacy Policy

ZoneClock does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.

- The app works entirely offline and makes no network connections.
- Saved cities and preferences are stored only on your device.
- There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party
  services.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/riverray121/ZoneClock/issues.
